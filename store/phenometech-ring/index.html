Another layer of data

More signal for your Longevity AI.

The ring streams heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, skin temperature and movement into Phenome's AI — so your microbiome and genome are read in the context of how you actually sleep, move and recover.

Heart rate

Read from the arteries in your finger, where the signal is stronger than at the wrist.

Heart-rate variability

The gap between one beat and the next, and the backbone of your readiness score.

Blood oxygen

Overnight SpO₂, read against the sleep stages recorded beside it rather than on its own.

Skin temperature

A dedicated sensor against the skin. What matters is the deviation from your own baseline.

Movement

A 3-axis accelerometer, separating a walk from a workout and a still night from a restless one.
