Designed in Sweden, made in China. Two year warranty.

01

Wear it sensor inwards

Turn the ring so the sensor sits against the inside of your finger. We suggest the index finger, where the arterial flow is strongest and the reading steadiest.

02

Charge it magnetically

Line the magnetic pin up with the port on the ring. A red light blinking once a second means it is charging, a steady green light means it is done.

03

Pair it in the app

Open Phenome Longevity, sign in or create an account, then allow the ring to sync. It begins matching your health data in real time.

The PhenomeTech Ring is a wellness device, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Speak to a qualified clinician about anything that concerns you.
