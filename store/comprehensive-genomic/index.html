Common questions.

What does the test analyse?
Your entire genome — all three billion base pairs — rather than a preselected subset of markers. We report the variants that carry evidence for health, longevity, metabolism and medication response across twelve panels.
How is it different from consumer DNA kits?
Consumer kits use a genotyping chip, which checks a fixed list of roughly half a million positions and infers the rest statistically. This is whole genome sequencing: every base is read directly, at 30× depth, so rare and novel variants that a chip has no probe for are still visible. It is run in an accredited laboratory and reported to clinical standards, and it comes with a genetic counsellor rather than a dashboard alone.
Does it cover disease risk and carrier status?
Both. Predisposition covers cardiac, metabolic, neurological, immune and cancer-related variants; carrier screening covers more than 300 recessive and X-linked conditions, which is the part that matters if you are planning a family. Clinically actionable findings are flagged separately so they are not lost among the wellness results.
Will I get medication (pharmacogenomic) insights?
Yes. The report covers the drug-metabolising enzymes with established prescribing guidance — among them CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and DPYD — and explains what each result means for dosing. It is written so you can hand it to your GP or consultant, but it does not replace their advice, and no medication should be started or stopped on the strength of it alone.
How do I collect my saliva sample?
A simple saliva sample, collected at home. Detailed instructions are printed in the kit: avoid eating, drinking and brushing for thirty minutes beforehand, fill the tube to the marked line, seal it, and post it back in the prepaid envelope supplied.
Can my data be re-analysed as science advances?
That is the argument for sequencing the whole genome once rather than genotyping repeatedly. Your raw sequence stays on file, so when a gene–condition association reaches clinical evidence we can revisit your existing data instead of asking for a new sample. You can request a re-analysis, or a copy of your raw data, at any time from your account.
How long until my results are ready?
Two to three weeks from the day your sample reaches the laboratory. You are notified when it arrives, and again when the report is released in the Phenome app; the counselling session can be booked as soon as the report is live.
Is my genetic data kept private?
Your sequence is stored encrypted in the UK and processed under GDPR as special category data. It is never sold, never shared with insurers or employers, and is used for research only if you opt in separately — that consent is not part of buying the test, and you can withdraw it later. You can export or permanently delete your data from your account at any time.
