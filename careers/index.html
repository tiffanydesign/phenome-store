CAREERS

Most of this company is still being decided.

We are young, we have changed shape twice in three years, and there is more here to build than there is built. That is either the reason to come or the reason to stay away, and it is worth knowing which before you write to us.

What the work actually is.

Reading several layers of a person’s biology and turning them into one plain answer they can act on this week.

That one sentence quietly contains a laboratory, a ring worn on a finger, a supplement formula, an app, and someone qualified to sit with a person and explain a result. All of it already exists here, under one roof, which is rarer than it sounds. Almost nothing about how the pieces fit together is finished — the hard, interesting problem is the joins, and the joins are where most of the work is.

If you want a defined role inside a system somebody else has already settled, this will frustrate you honestly and quickly. If you would rather help settle it, that is the job.

The kinds of work this company contains.

These are disciplines, not vacancies. We do not keep a list of open roles on this page — write to us about the one you would want to do, and we will tell you honestly whether it exists yet.

Laboratory and genomics

Sequencing, metagenomics, variant interpretation, and the quiet discipline of a result being right before it is fast.

Software and the app

Every report a person receives arrives through the app. It is the product, not a companion to it.

Clinical and nutrition

Genetic counselling and nutrition consultancy — the part of this business where a person talks to a person.

Design, writing and photography

Making something warm and legible out of a subject the whole category insists on making cold.

Operations and support

Kits, logistics, turnaround, and answering the person who is waiting for a result they care about.

Research

Population-scale work that improves the interpretation every customer gets back. The long game.

HOW WE WORK

Three rules that decide most arguments.

We sell life, not science.

The accreditations and the sequencing depth are load-bearing, and they are not the promise. What somebody actually buys from us is a better Tuesday. Work that forgets this gets sent back.

Warm, never clinical.

The default aesthetic for anything genomic is stainless steel and blue-white light. We go the other way in everything we make, including the London clinic, which is briefed to feel residential.

Claim nothing that can’t be checked.

One unverifiable sentence on a health brand costs more than ten true ones earn. When a claim can’t be substantiated we cut it rather than soften it. Nobody here has to argue for that.

If you want to write.

There is no form, no portal and no reference number. An email is fine.

What to send

Say what you would want to work on here and what you have already done that resembles it. Links are more useful to us than attachments. Two paragraphs beats two pages.

Where to send it

careers@phenomelongevity.com — we read what arrives, and we will not pretend to answer everything the same week.

Or use the general contact route

An open invitation, and a real inbox at the end of it.
