Frequently asked questions

Is the Phenome app free?

Yes. The app is free with any Phenome test or the PhenomeTech Ring. There is no subscription and nothing to pay to unlock a feature.

Do I need a PhenomeTech Ring to use the app?

No. Your results, reports and Longevity AI work on their own. Adding the Ring or the Band brings in sleep, heart rate and activity, so your insight updates day to day.

Which phones does the app work on?

iPhone with iOS 16 or later, and Android phones with Android 10 or later.

How does Longevity AI use my data?

It answers from your own genome, microbiome and Ring readings first, then from the research behind them. It is for wellness and information, and is not a diagnosis.

Can I share my results with my doctor?

Yes. Download any report as a PDF, send a summary, or book a session with a nutrition specialist or genetic counsellor from the app.

What happens to my data if I stop using the app?

It stays yours. Export it or delete it whenever you choose. We never sell it or share it without your consent.
