Why a building

Why a testing companyIs building a room.

Until now we have been a company you order from. A kit arrives, you post it back, a report appears in the app. That works, and it will keep working.

But there is a kind of trust that does not travel through a letterbox.

A clinic turns a mail order testing brand into somewhere you can walk into, sit down in, and leave from having spoken to a person. We think that is worth a building, and we would rather admit that we are still constructing it than describe one that isn’t there.

The temperature

Warm, not clinical.

The interior brief asks for one thing above everything else: a warm, residential, restorative feel. That is the same instruction the rest of this company works to, written down for a builder instead of a designer.

Residential, not reception

Rooms briefed to feel domestic. Somewhere you sit down, rather than somewhere you wait.

Restorative, not efficient

Built around having enough time. The useful part of a result is the conversation about it, and that part cannot be rushed.

Not a cold laboratory

The default look for anything genomic is steel and strip light. This is deliberately the opposite. The sequencing still happens in a lab, it just doesn’t happen here.

Honest answers

Questions

Is the clinic open?

No. It is in development in London. The day that changes, it will say so at the top of this page.

Where will it be?

London. We are not printing a street address until there is one you can turn up to.

When does it open?

There is no public date. Register your interest and we will write to you when there is one, not before.

What will it cost?

There is no price list yet. When there is, it will be published here rather than quoted on request.

Do I need to have tested with Phenome first?

No. Having results makes the first conversation more useful, because there is something to read together. It is not a condition of coming in.

Can I speak to someone before it opens?

Yes. Consultations run remotely today, you can book a session in the app, or contact the team.

Is this medical care?

No. Phenome tests are for wellness and information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Anything that worries you belongs with your GP.
