The visit

Six parts, in order.

Nothing here is complicated. That is the point.

01Before you come

We ask what you want out of the appointment. If you already have results with us, they are read before you arrive, so the time is spent on you rather than on someone opening a file in front of you.

02Arriving

A door, a room, a chair. No clipboard on the way in. Anything we need to ask, we ask sitting down, with a drink, in a room that is meant to feel like a room.

03The conversation

The longest part, deliberately. What your results say, what they do not say, and which of it is worth doing something about this month. You are allowed to disagree, and to ask the same question twice.

04If a sample is taken

Saliva or a swab, taken here instead of at your kitchen table, then sent to the same accredited lab that processes every kit we post. Nothing is analysed on the premises.

05What you leave with

A short written summary of what was said and what to do next, in plain language, in the app. Not a folder of numbers you will never open again.

06Afterwards

Whatever was agreed continues where your results already live. If a supplement changes, it changes there. If something needs a doctor, we will say so plainly rather than hint at it.

Take this with you

What to bring.

Short list, and none of it is compulsory.

Anything already written about you

A blood panel, a letter from another clinician, an old report from anywhere. Read alongside your results, it makes the hour better.

What you actually take

Medication, supplements, the thing you take some weeks and not others. Honesty here changes the advice more than any single test result does.

Someone, if you want

Family and carrier results are usually easier to hear with the other person in the room. Bring them.

Being clear

What it will not be.

Three things are worth saying before they are asked.

  • Not a diagnosis

    Phenome tests are for wellness and information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Where something should be looked at by a doctor, we will tell you so.

  • Not a sales appointment

    You will not need to buy anything on the day. Anything suggested can be read, thought about, and ordered later, or not.

  • Not a replacement for your GP

    Your report is written so that another clinician can read it. Taking it to yours is encouraged, not discouraged.

Practical

Questions

A page like this usually pretends to have every answer. Here is where ours run out.

How long is an appointment?

Not fixed yet. “Long enough to read a report properly” is the brief we are designing to, and we would rather set the number once the rooms exist than promise one now.

How do I get there?

There is no address to publish yet. When there is, directions and the nearest stations go here.

Is the building accessible?

The fit out is still being specified. The access detail will be published here before we open, not after.

Can I come without having tested?

Yes. There is simply less to read together on the first visit. If you would like to test first, comparing the tests will point you at the right one.

Can I do all of this remotely instead?

Yes, today. Consultations run over video, book a session, and every report is delivered in the app whether or not you ever come in.
