YOUR REPORT

What’s included in your Comprehensive Genomic Report

A complete analysis of your DNA across 13 health panels — covering disease risk, medication response, metabolism, longevity, performance, brain health, hormones, and carrier status. Each panel translates complex genomic data into clear, actionable insights.

  • Personalised Genomic Summary

    A plain-language overview and interpretation of your whole report, drawing together the key findings across all panels and what they mean for you.

  • Disease Predisposition & Risk Analysis

    Genetic variants associated with complex and monogenic conditions, each classified by clinical relevance and inheritance pattern, so you can see which areas may need preventive monitoring or professional advice.

  • Carrier Screening & Inherited Variants

    Whether you carry genetic variants that could be passed to your children, supporting informed family planning.

  • Pharmacogenomic Insights

    How your genes influence your response to medications — antidepressants, cardiovascular drugs, anticoagulants, pain relief, and metabolic agents — helping professionals choose the safest, most effective options.

  • Nutrigenomic & Metabolic Health

    How your body metabolises nutrients and responds to different diets, including vitamin absorption, caffeine and lactose metabolism, fat storage, weight regulation, and insulin response.

  • Lifestyle & Wellness Genetics

    Genetic traits that influence diet, metabolism, and lifestyle response, with insight into nutrient processing, exercise response, and sleep regulation.

  • Longevity & Ageing Pathways

    Genes involved in cellular repair, inflammation control, and antioxidant activity that influence ageing rate, metabolic health, and lifespan potential.

  • Mental & Cognitive Health

    Genes associated with neurological, cognitive, and psychological function — including influences on mood, memory, focus, and certain neuropsychiatric traits.

  • Sports & Exercise Genetics

    Traits affecting strength, endurance, muscle recovery, and injury susceptibility, to help optimise training and long-term fitness.

  • Hormonal & Reproductive Health

    Insights into hormone metabolism, fertility, and reproductive wellbeing, including PCOS, ovarian reserve, sperm quality, and hormonal balance.

  • Cardiovascular & Lipid Metabolism

    Genes associated with heart health, cholesterol, and blood pressure, identifying hereditary cardiovascular risks and supporting prevention.

  • Mitochondrial & Energy Function

    Genes involved in mitochondrial performance and energy efficiency, helping explain potential causes of fatigue or low energy production.

  • Detoxification & Environmental Response

    How your genes process and eliminate toxins from the environment, alcohol, and drugs, and your sensitivity to pollutants, oxidative stress, and chemical exposures.

FAQ

Common questions

What does the Comprehensive Genomic Test analyse?
It examines your entire genome to identify genetic variants that influence health, longevity, metabolism, and response to medication — across disease risk, carrier status, pharmacogenomics, and ageing pathways.
How is this different from other genetic tests?
Unlike targeted panels that focus on specific genes, this test uses whole genome sequencing to analyse all coding and non-coding regions of your DNA, for a deeper and more complete understanding.
Who should take this test?
Suitable for anyone over 18 wanting a complete overview of their genetic health — for proactive healthcare, personalised nutrition, or understanding inherited risks before planning a family.
How is the sample collected?
A simple saliva sample. Detailed collection instructions are provided in the kit for easy, accurate collection at home.
How accurate are the results?
Next-generation sequencing with high read depth achieves over 99% accuracy for variant detection. All results are reviewed and interpreted by certified genetic specialists.
How long does it take?
Typically three to four weeks from when the sample reaches the laboratory. You’ll receive a digital report once analysis and quality checks are complete.
Where can I see my results?
Uploaded automatically to your secure profile in the app, created with the email you provided at checkout, and downloadable as a PDF anytime.
What should I do after receiving my report?
If the report identifies variants of clinical relevance, you’re encouraged to book a genetic counselling session to understand your results and next steps.
