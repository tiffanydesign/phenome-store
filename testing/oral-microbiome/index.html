YOUR REPORT

What is included in your Oral Microbiome Test Report

Your report analyses bacterial balance, diversity, and the presence of key species associated with oral and systemic health. Each section expands to help you interpret your results.

  • Overview Summary

    A snapshot of your oral microbiome balance and overall health score, compared to reference values from healthy populations.

  • Richness & Diversity

    How many bacterial species exist in your oral microbiome and how evenly they are distributed. A diverse microbiome protects against inflammation and disease.

  • Microbial Composition

    Analysis of major bacterial phyla (Firmicutes, Bacteroidetes, Proteobacteria, Actinobacteria, Fusobacteria), showing how your profile compares to optimal ranges.

  • Firmicutes to Bacteroidetes Ratio

    A marker of microbial equilibrium, linked to weight regulation, metabolism, and oral inflammation.

  • Key Oral Health Indicators

    Results on specific bacteria related to common conditions including caries, gingivitis, halitosis, and periodontitis.

  • Oral-Systemic Health Connections

    Bacterial groups associated with systemic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and inflammatory disorders.

  • Inflammatory Potential

    Bacteria involved in inflammation and immune response within the mouth and throughout the body.

  • Protective and Harmful Species

    Beneficial bacteria that defend oral health, and potential pathogens that may contribute to disease risk.

  • Personalised Recommendations

    Targeted guidance on oral hygiene, diet, supplements, and lifestyle to help restore balance and improve oral and systemic wellbeing.

FAQ

Common questions

What does the Oral Microbiome Test actually analyse?
It examines the bacterial composition of your mouth, identifying species that affect oral and systemic health, and detects imbalances linked to gum disease, bad breath, tooth decay, and inflammation.
Why is the oral microbiome important?
The mouth is the gateway to the body — oral bacteria can influence not only dental health but also heart, gut, and immune function. A balanced oral microbiome supports both oral hygiene and general wellbeing.
How is the sample collected?
The test uses a simple saliva or oral swab sample. It is painless, quick, and collected at home using the instructions in the kit.
What kind of insights will I receive?
Bacterial composition, species diversity, and the Firmicutes-to-Bacteroidetes ratio, along with findings on gum health, inflammation, and overall microbial balance.
How accurate is the analysis?
The test uses metagenomic sequencing, identifying bacteria at species level with high precision and reliability.
Who should consider taking this test?
Anyone experiencing symptoms such as bad breath, gum irritation, or frequent mouth infections, and those wanting to prevent oral and systemic issues.
When should I take and send my sample?
Collect Monday–Thursday and return the same day using the prepaid envelope, ideally before midday. Avoid public holidays.
Where can I see my results?
Uploaded automatically to your secure profile in the app, with a PDF you can download anytime.
