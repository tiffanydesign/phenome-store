The basicsWhat a gene is

A gene is a small section of your DNA that tells your body how to grow, heal, and function, from how your cells repair to how your metabolism and immunity work. Together, your genes form the complete blueprint of your health. Everyone’s genes differ, shaping everything from inherited traits to performance potential.

Most tests read a fraction.We read all of it.

Every cell in your body contains DNA, an instruction manual written in about three billion letters. Most genetic tests read only small parts of it. Whole Genome Sequencing reads all of it, so we can see every piece of genetic information that makes you who you are. It also means your data can be examined again as the science advances.
