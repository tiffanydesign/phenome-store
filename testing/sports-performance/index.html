WHY IT MATTERS

Why sports genetics improves your results.

Every body responds to training differently. Genetic variation shapes how you build muscle, sustain endurance, and recover — helping explain why the same programme delivers different results for different people.

Power, Strength & Endurance

How your muscles generate speed and power, and how efficiently oxygen reaches them during exercise. — Genes: ACTN3, ACE, PPARGC1A, VEGFA

Energy & Metabolism

How your body fuels activity, processes nutrients, and uses fat and carbohydrate for energy. — Genes: PPARA, CPT1B, FTO, ADRB2, MTHFR, SOD2, GSTT1

Injury Risk & Recovery

How your tendons and ligaments handle load, and how strongly your body manages inflammation after training. — Genes: COL5A1, COL1A1, COL3A1, GDF5, IL6, IL1B, TNF

Mind & Motivation

How your genes shape drive, focus, and the way you respond to competition and stress. — Genes: BDNF, COMT, DRD2, SLC6A4

Your results are a guide, not a verdict. Genes reveal tendencies — training, nutrition, and consistency decide how far you take them.

What does the Sports Performance Test analyse?
The test examines genes that influence strength, endurance, recovery, and injury risk — helping you understand how your body responds to different types of exercise and training intensity.
Who can benefit from this test?
Anyone over 18 interested in improving physical performance — from professional athletes to beginners. Results help tailor training, recovery, and nutrition to your genetic profile.
How accurate are the results?
The analysis uses advanced next-generation sequencing with high accuracy. All findings are reviewed by certified genetic specialists to ensure reliable interpretation.
What kind of insights will I receive?
Insights on muscle composition, energy metabolism, recovery rate, tendon and ligament strength, and your response to endurance or power training.
How can this test improve my performance?
By understanding your genetic strengths and recovery capacity, you can build a training plan that maximises results, reduces injury risk, and supports long-term goals.
How is the sample collected?
A simple saliva sample, collected easily at home using the instructions provided in the kit.
When should I take and send my sample?
Collect between Monday and Thursday and return it the same day using the prepaid envelope, ideally before midday. Avoid public holidays to prevent delays.
Where can I see my results?
Uploaded automatically to your secure profile in the app, with an email link, and downloadable as a PDF anytime.
Do I need professional guidance to interpret my results?
Your report is easy to understand, but you can also discuss it with a coach, physiologist, or genetic counsellor to integrate the insights into your plan.
