WHY IT MATTERS

Gut microbiome testing: why it matters for digestive health.

The gut microbiome is a central regulator of human health, influencing digestion, immune function, metabolism, energy levels, and brain–gut signalling linked to mood and cognitive clarity. When balanced, these systems function optimally. When disrupted (microbial dysbiosis), measurable imbalances can affect multiple aspects of daily health.

THE FOUR CORE JOBS

What your gut microbiome does

Digestion & Nutrients

Breaks down fibres the body can’t, turning them into short-chain fatty acids, and produces vitamins like K, folate, and B vitamins.

Immunity & Defence

Around 70% of immune cells live in the gut. A balanced microbiome trains them to tell friend from threat and keeps inflammation in check.

Hormones & Metabolism

More than 20 hormones originate in the gut, including serotonin, ghrelin, and GLP-1, shaping appetite, mood, and blood-sugar control.

The Gut-Brain Axis

Gut microbes help produce serotonin and dopamine, so a balanced gut tends to mean clearer mood, better sleep, and steadier energy.

Common questions

What does the Gut Microbiome Test analyse?
It analyses the composition and balance of your gut microbiome, identifying key bacterial species, diversity levels, and patterns associated with digestion, immunity, metabolism, and overall wellbeing.
Why is gut health important?
The gut microbiome influences nutrient absorption, hormone regulation, immune function, and mood. Understanding its composition enables targeted dietary and lifestyle changes that support long-term health.
How is the sample collected?
You collect a small stool sample at home using the provided sterile kit, with step-by-step instructions for safe, hygienic collection.
What insights will I receive in the report?
A detailed overview of bacterial diversity, abundance, and balance, with findings on digestion, inflammation, and microbial composition, plus personalised recommendations.
How accurate is the analysis?
The test uses shotgun metagenomic sequencing for high-depth, high-precision analysis, identifying bacteria at both genus and species level.
Who should take this test?
Adults 18 and over wanting to understand their gut health, particularly those experiencing digestive discomfort, bloating, low energy, or irregular bowel habits.
When should I take and send my sample?
Collect between Monday and Thursday (Royal Mail days) and return the same day using the prepaid envelope, ideally before midday. Avoid public holidays.
Where can I see my results?
Results are uploaded automatically to your secure profile in the app, created with the email you provided at checkout. You can download the report as a PDF anytime.
