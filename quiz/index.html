How the plan is built

From answers to a personalised plan.

Each answer adds weight

Every option you choose adds weight to one or more products. Nothing is hidden — the reasoning is shown back to you at the end.

The highest weight becomes the hero

The highest-weighted product becomes your primary recommendation; the next one or two become supporting add-ons.

Family signals surface screening

Family-planning answers surface Carrier Screening, and Newborn Screening where relevant.

Support is opt-in

Saying yes to ongoing support adds Personalised Supplements; saying no keeps it to products only.
