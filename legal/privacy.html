Privacy & data

Phenome processes personal and genetic data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This page summarises what we collect, why, and the control you retain over it.

What we collect. Account details, order information, the biological sample you send, the sequencing data derived from it, and app usage data. Wearable data if you use a PhenomeTech Ring.

Why we process it. To deliver the test you purchased, to produce and review your report, and to support you afterwards. We do not use your genetic data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Storage and security. Sequencing data is stored encrypted at rest in the UK and EU, with access limited to the clinical and laboratory staff who need it. Samples are destroyed after the analysis period unless you ask us to retain them.

Sharing. We share data with the accredited laboratory processing your sample and, where you request it, with a clinician you nominate. We never share with insurers, employers or advertisers.

Your rights. Access, rectification, portability, restriction and erasure. Request deletion from the app or by contacting support; we action it and confirm in writing.

Contact. For any data question, contact our Data Protection Officer through the support page.
