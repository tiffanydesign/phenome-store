Science, multiomics

Your biology isn’t one datasetIt’s many, in conversation

Your genome is your blueprint. Your microbiome is your second genome. Your proteins and gene expression show what’s happening right now, and your wearable tracks how you actually live. Multiomics reads these layers together, because the signal that matters lives in how they interact, not in any one of them alone.

The layers we combine

Each layer answers a different question. Together they explain not just what’s happening, but why, and what to do about it.

  1. Genome

    Your inherited blueprint, the potential you were born with. It rarely changes, so it’s the stable backbone every other layer is read against.

  2. Microbiome

    The trillions of microbes in your gut and mouth, your “second genome.” It shifts with diet and lifestyle and shapes immunity, metabolism and mood.

  3. Proteome and transcriptome

    Proteins and gene expression reveal what your genes are actually doing right now, bridging fixed DNA and day to day physiology.

  4. Wearable signals

    Continuous sleep, heart rate, HRV and activity from the Ring and Band, the lived reality your biology plays out in.

  5. Clinical data

    Your labs, history and measurements, brought in so insights are grounded in your real clinical picture.

Why one layer isn’t enough

A single test tells a partial story. Your genome shows what could happen; your microbiome and wearables show what is happening. Cause and action only appear when you read them together.

Genome alone

= potential

Tells you predisposition, but not whether it’s expressed today or what’s driving how you feel.

Microbiome / wearable alone

= snapshot

Shows your current state, but not the underlying blueprint or why it’s trending that way.

Together

= cause and action

Layered, your data explains the mechanism, and points to the specific, personalised thing to change.
