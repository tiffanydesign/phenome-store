The name is the argument.

Most people read Phenome as a stylish misspelling of genome. It isn’t. It is a real word, and it happens to contain the whole reason this company exists.

Your genome

The code you were born with. It never changes — the same in a cell taken from you at birth as in one taken at eighty. Read it once and it is yours for life.

Your phenome

Everything that code actually became. Your resting heart rate this morning. The bacteria in your gut this month. How well you slept last night. The genome after years of food, sleep, stress, medication and exercise have had their way with it.

That difference has a practical consequence, and everything here rests on it. The genome takes a sequencer. The gut takes metagenomics. Sleep and heart rhythm take something worn on a finger, night after night. Each of those instruments is genuinely good at its one layer, and blind to every other. So we run several at once.

WHAT IT IS FOR

One ordinary question.

Someone asks the app why their energy has dipped this month. Here is the shape of an answer that needs more than one instrument to give.

The question

“Why has my energy dipped this month?”

The answer

“Gut diversity is down, B12 is low-normal, and sleep efficiency has been slipping — together they explain the dip. Here’s a two-week plan to bring it back.”

Three separate layers — gut, blood and what you wore on your finger — one conclusion, and something to do on Monday. A wearable on its own can tell you your sleep efficiency slipped. It cannot tell you why, because the why is in your gut and your blood. A genetic test can tell you what you are predisposed to; it cannot tell you what happened this month. Our own shorthand for the difference is signal and source: a ring shows you what, your genome, blood and gut reveal why.

The exchange above is an illustration of how the app answers, not a customer’s result. Phenome tests are for wellness and information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

What sits behind it.

The science is not the shop window. It is the thing holding the shop window up — permanently available, and not the point.

The laboratory is ours

Samples are processed at SZAOmics, our own laboratory, which holds CAP, CLIA and ISO accreditation. When something needs checking, we can walk to the bench and ask.

The expensive method, on purpose

Genetic tests are read by whole-genome sequencing. Microbiome samples are read by shotgun metagenomics rather than 16S. 16S reads one marker gene and usually gets you as far as the genus; shotgun reads everything in the sample and resolves species, and what they are capable of doing. It costs considerably more.

One place for the results

Every report is delivered in the free Phenome app, on iOS and Android, next to the assistant that reads your layers together and answers questions in plain language.

There is no subscription

You pay once, for the object or the test. Reading your own data does not cost anything per month. In a category built on recurring revenue this is a deliberate position, and it is the one we get asked about most.

What we won’t claim
