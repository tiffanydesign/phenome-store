Questions

Is the Band suited to exercise?

Yes, it is built for movement. Runs, strength sessions, swims and rides are detected on their own, and each one is logged with heart rate zones, training load and the recovery time it asks for.

Does it have a screen?

No. With nothing to light up the battery lasts about a week, and your day is read in the app, where there is room to explain it.

Is there a subscription?

No. The app, your insights and future updates come with the Band.

Should I choose the Band or the Ring?

The Ring is the better sleep tracker, since it is barely felt overnight. The Band is the better training partner, with a firmer fit through hard effort. Both feed the same account, and the app merges the two without counting anything twice.

Which strap size do I need?

Every box holds both sizes, small to medium for wrists of 130 to 170 mm and medium to large for 160 to 210 mm. Wear it snug enough not to slide, loose enough to fit a finger underneath.

How does the Band use my genome?

If you have taken a Phenome genomic test, your results set the baseline your readings are compared with, such as how quickly you clear caffeine or how you respond to endurance training. The Band still works on its own from the first night.

Is the Band a medical device?

No. It is a wellness device, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to a clinician about anything that concerns you.
