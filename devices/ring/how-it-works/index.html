How it works

From your fingerTo your phone

Sensors read your body around the clock, the app turns them into scores, and Phenome connects them to the bigger picture.

The sensors

A lab on your finger.

Optical heart sensor

Green and red LEDs read your pulse for heart rate, HRV and blood oxygen.

Temperature sensor

Tracks skin temperature and nightly deltas against your personal baseline.

Motion sensor

A 3 axis accelerometer captures steps, activity and movement in sleep.

Getting connected

Up and running in minutes.

No wires, no cables to the computer, just your phone and Bluetooth.

01Download the app

Get the Phenome app free from the App Store or Google Play, and create your account.

02Pair your ring

Open the app and hold the ring close to your phone, it connects over Bluetooth in seconds.

03Wear and sync

Slip it on and it starts reading straight away. Data syncs automatically whenever you open the app.
