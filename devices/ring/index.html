Sleep score

Your stages, your lowest heart rate and your temperature through the night become one score each morning, with the factors that moved it, so you know what to change tonight.

Recovery

Read while you sleep and measured against what is normal for you, it tells you how ready your body is before the day asks anything of it: push, hold, or rest.

Stress balance

Through the day your heart shows when you are calm, engaged or stretched. See which moments wind you up, and which bring you back down fastest.

Breathing at night

Oxygen and breathing rate followed across every night, with any dip outside your usual range flagged the next morning, so a restless night has a reason.

Temperature trend

Small nightly shifts drawn over weeks rather than read one at a time, so a real change stands out from the noise, and a cycle can be followed month to month.

Training load

How far you moved, weighed against what your heart did while you moved, so you see how hard a day really was and how long it will take to come back from.
