Questions?We’re here to help

When will I notice a difference?

Most customers report better energy and sleep within 2 to 4 weeks, and clearer focus within 3 to 5 weeks. Take one sachet each morning.

How is this different from precursor supplements?

Precursors such as nicotinamide mononucleotide and nicotinamide riboside have to be converted to NAD+ by your body, less efficiently with age. Phenome+ NAD+ is the finished molecule, delivered directly.

Can NAD+ really be absorbed orally?

Yes. The liposomal format protects it through digestion and delivers it into the bloodstream intact, achieving plasma levels tablets can’t match.

How do I take it?

Take one 10ml sachet each morning. There is nothing to mix and nothing to swallow whole.

Can I take it with my other supplements?

Many customers do. If you take prescription medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, speak to your doctor before starting any new supplement.
