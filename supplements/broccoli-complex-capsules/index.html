The scienceBehind it

Sulforaphane precursors found in broccoli activate the Nrf2 antioxidant pathway, a master regulator responsible for reducing oxidative stress, supporting detoxification enzymes, and protecting cell integrity. Research shows that Nrf2 activation plays a crucial role in longevity, immune regulation, and overall metabolic health.

  1. 01

    Glucoraphanin

    Broccoli is one of the richest natural sources of glucoraphanin, a compound converted by the enzyme myrosinase into sulforaphane, one of the most extensively studied phytonutrients in nutritional science.

  2. 02

    Nrf2

    Sulforaphane is best known for activating the Nrf2 (Nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2) pathway, the body’s master regulator of antioxidant and detoxification gene expression.

  3. 03

    Protective enzymes

    When Nrf2 is activated, it triggers the production of phase II detoxification enzymes including glutathione S-transferase, quinone reductase, and haem oxygenase, enzymes that neutralise reactive oxygen species and environmental carcinogens, protect DNA from oxidative damage, and support immune pathway regulation.

Sulforaphane has been investigated in clinical trials across a range of conditions including metabolic syndrome, cardiovascular protection, and neuroinflammation. Alpha lipoic acid is a mitochondrial antioxidant active in both water soluble and fat soluble environments, complementing sulforaphane’s antioxidant effects by regenerating vitamins C and E. Cinnamon extract supports insulin signalling and healthy post meal blood glucose regulation. Magnesium taurate supports neuromuscular function and energy metabolism. Chromium contributes to normal macronutrient metabolism, a permitted EU health claim.

Questions

What is broccoli extract and how is it different from eating broccoli?

Broccoli extract is a concentrated, standardised form of glucoraphanin, the precursor compound that converts into sulforaphane in the body. Unlike eating fresh broccoli, a standardised extract guarantees a consistent and meaningful dose of glucoraphanin with every serving. Cooking broccoli destroys much of its myrosinase enzyme activity, which is required for glucoraphanin to sulforaphane conversion, a limitation that does not apply to our supplement format.

What is sulforaphane and what does it do?

Sulforaphane is a naturally occurring compound produced when glucoraphanin reacts with the enzyme myrosinase. It is one of the most studied phytonutrients in nutritional science, primarily known for activating the Nrf2 pathway, the body’s master regulator of antioxidant and detoxification gene expression. Nrf2 activation triggers the production of protective enzymes that neutralise oxidative stress, support liver detoxification, and regulate immune pathways.

Why does this formula include other ingredients alongside broccoli extract?

Each ingredient in this formula is included for a specific reason. Cinnamon extract and chromium support healthy insulin signalling and post meal blood glucose balance. Alpha lipoic acid is a mitochondrial antioxidant that works in both water soluble and fat soluble environments, extending antioxidant protection. Magnesium taurate supports neuromuscular function and energy production. Together these ingredients create a more complete metabolic and antioxidant support formula than broccoli extract alone.

Is this product suitable for vegans?

Yes. The formula, capsule shell, and all excipients are vegan. The product is also non GMO and free from gluten, dairy, soy, and artificial additives.

How many capsules should I take per day?

The recommended serving is 2 capsules per day, taken with food. This provides the full daily dose of each ingredient as listed on the label. Do not exceed this amount.

When is the best time to take Broccoli Complex?

Take both capsules with a meal, breakfast or lunch is ideal. Taking with food helps maximise absorption and reduces the small chance of digestive discomfort that can occasionally occur with concentrated plant extracts taken on an empty stomach.

How long before I notice any effects?

Most people following a consistent daily routine notice changes in energy levels and general wellbeing within two to four weeks. The antioxidant and detoxification effects of sulforaphane are cumulative, daily consistency over weeks and months produces greater benefit than short term supplementation.

Is Broccoli Complex safe to take during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

We recommend consulting a healthcare professional before taking any food supplement during pregnancy or breastfeeding. While broccoli extract is derived from a food source, the concentrated doses in a supplement are higher than those typically consumed through diet, and individual circumstances vary.

Can children take this product?

This product is not designed for children unless medically advised. All dosing on the label is calculated for adults.

Are there any known interactions with medications?

Concentrated broccoli extract may interact with blood thinning medications such as warfarin, as broccoli contains vitamin K related compounds. Alpha lipoic acid may affect blood glucose levels and should be used with caution if you are taking diabetes medication. If you are taking any prescription medication, consult your GP or pharmacist before use.

Does this product contain any allergens?

This product is free from the 14 major allergens listed under UK food labelling regulations. It is also GMO free.

What form of magnesium is used and why?

We use magnesium taurate, a chelated form of magnesium bonded to the amino acid taurine. Magnesium taurate is associated with particularly good cardiovascular and neurological tolerability compared to cheaper forms such as magnesium oxide, which has poor bioavailability and is more likely to cause digestive discomfort.

What is alpha lipoic acid and why is it included?

Alpha lipoic acid (ALA) is a naturally occurring compound and mitochondrial antioxidant. It is uniquely able to function in both water soluble and fat soluble biological environments, giving it broader antioxidant reach than most other antioxidants. ALA also regenerates other antioxidants including vitamins C and E, making it a synergistic addition to the broccoli extract’s Nrf2 activating effects.

Why is chromium included in this formula?

Chromium contributes to normal macronutrient metabolism, a permitted EU health claim. At 180 micrograms per serving it is within the safe and effective range established in the scientific literature for supporting insulin receptor function and the normal metabolism of carbohydrates, fats, and proteins.
