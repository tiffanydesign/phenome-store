Order questions

I haven’t had a dispatch email
Check the folder your confirmation email landed in first. If it isn’t there, the order hasn’t been dispatched yet — message support with your order number and we’ll tell you where it stands.
Can I change the delivery address?
Message support with your order number as soon as you can. If it hasn’t been dispatched, we can usually change it; once it’s on its way, we can’t.
Do you deliver outside the UK?
The delivery times and charges on this page are for the UK. For anywhere else, message support before you order and we’ll tell you what’s possible.
My order arrived, but the results are late
Those are two different clocks. Results are ready 2–3 weeks after the lab receives your sample — and the lab can’t start until the kit is activated. If it’s been longer, message support.
Something arrived damaged
Tell us which order it was. If a kit is damaged or a sample fails, we send a replacement at no cost and prioritise the re-run.
