What an account holds

Four things, and they only mean something together.

Your results

Every test you’ve taken, kept in one place, genome, gut, oral, and whatever your Ring recorded while you slept. Not six PDFs lost in an inbox.

Your reports

The written version: reviewed by a clinician, put in plain language, and downloadable as a PDF for the day you need to hand it to someone.

Your plan

What to actually do on a Tuesday, the formula built against your own results, the change worth making, the session with a specialist when you want one.

Your orders

What you’ve ordered, what’s on its way, and what’s still sitting in a box in the hallway waiting to be activated.

Questions

I don’t remember which email I used

Try the address the order confirmation arrived at. If that draws a blank, message support with your name and roughly when you ordered, and we’ll find it.

Do I need an account to use the PhenomeTech Ring?

Yes. The Ring pairs with the Phenome app, and the app is where sleep, heart rate, HRV and activity are read. There is no monthly fee to see your own data.

Can two people share one account?

No, an account holds one person’s results, and mixing samples into one profile makes every reading harder to trust. Order separately, and each account keeps its own results.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Privacy → Delete account in the app, or message support. Your data is removed and we confirm it in writing.

Something isn’t working

Message support with what you were trying to do and, if it concerns an order, the order number. A person reads it.
