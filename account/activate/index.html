Why one scan matters this much

It gives the sample a name

An unactivated sample arrives at the lab belonging to nobody. We won’t report on something we can’t tie to a person — which is why results cannot be released without it.

It puts the report where you’ll find it

Activation is what sends the finished report to your profile in the app. Results are ready 2–3 weeks after the lab receives your sample.

It’s the point of no return

Unopened kits can be returned within 30 days. Once a kit has been activated or a sample sent, it can’t be refunded — so activate the one you mean to use.

If something isn’t right

The code won’t scan
More light, and hold the phone still a moment longer. If it still won’t read, type the code in above, or message support with your order number.
The kit arrived damaged, or my sample failed
We send a replacement at no cost and prioritise the re-run. Message support and tell us which kit it was.
I’ve ordered more than one kit
Each kit has its own code, so activate them one at a time. That is what keeps the results apart when they come back.
I’m activating for a child
Newborn Screening is ordered by a parent or legal guardian, and it is activated from that same account.
I activated weeks ago and heard nothing
Turnaround runs from the lab receiving your sample, not from the day you activated. If it has been longer than 2–3 weeks since you posted it, message support and we’ll check the run.
